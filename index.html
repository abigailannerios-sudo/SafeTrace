Where Safety &
Support Meet.

Empowering MSU-IIT student boarders with essential relief coordination and verified support. Every contribution is accounted for and publicly tracked in real-time from pledge to delivery.

Student Boarding
Directory

Finding a secure and affordable place near MSU-IIT shouldn't be stressful. SafeStay Connect curates an authorized directory of accredited student boarding houses and dormitories—complete with verified safety amenities, emergency contact points, and verified landlord credentials to ensure every student boarder has a safe home away from home.

Community
Outreach &
Donation Hub

Beyond secure housing, we provide direct humanitarian support during displacements, fire incidents, and campus emergencies. Our community outreach tracks inbound relief supplies—from hygiene kits to food packs—with 100% open transparency, ensuring donations reach student boarders and evacuation centers with zero delay.

Executives

Vision

To build a nurturing environment where all student boarders can thrive—academically, socially, and emotionally—fostering a strong sense of community and mutual support within MSU-IIT.

Mission

  • Advocate for student boarders' rights and welfare
  • Provide essential support services for safety, housing, and mental well-being
  • Connect boarders with resources that enhance both academic and personal growth
